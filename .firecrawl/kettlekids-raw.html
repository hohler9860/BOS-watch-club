Nestled in the heart of Old Bond Street, Kettle Kids Townhouse is a curated space for collectors, connoisseurs, and the next generation of watch enthusiasts. Inside, you'll discover an evolving collection of rare, iconic, and investment-grade timepieces - each with a story, and each selected with intent. Whether you're a seasoned collector or just beginning your journey, our team is here to guide you through every detail.

Harvey & Jacob Hutson

Richard Mille

RM27-03

The Richard Mille RM 27-03 is defined by its 47.77 × 40.30 mm Quartz TPT® case, engineered to endure shocks of up to 10,000 g. Its manual-winding tourbillon movement, offering a 70-hour power reserve, is suspended within an intricately skeletonised structure, exemplifying extreme lightness and uncompromising performance.

Richard Mille

RM67-02

The Richard Mille RM 67-02 pairs a 47.52 × 38.70 mm Quartz TPT® or Carbon TPT® case with exceptional lightness and resilience. Driven by the ultra-thin automatic CRMA7 calibre, it delivers approximately 50 hours of power reserve, conceived for high-performance wear with effortless comfort.

Richard Mille

RM67-01

The Richard Mille RM 67-01 is presented in a 47.52 × 38.70 mm tonneau-shaped case, available in titanium, white gold, and rose gold. Powered by the ultra-thin automatic CRMA6 calibre with a 50-hour power reserve, it balances refined ergonomics with a distinctly modern, skeletonised aesthetic.

Richard Mille

RM 055

The Richard Mille RM 055 features a 49.90 × 42.70 mm case, most notably in ATZ white ceramic or Carbon TPT®. Its manual-winding RMUL2 tourbillon movement, built on a grade 5 titanium baseplate, offers a 55-hour power reserve and is engineered for exceptional shock resistance in demanding environments.

Patek Philippe

5271P-010

The Patek Philippe 5271P-010 showcases a 41 mm platinum case set with baguette-cut diamonds. It is powered by the manually wound CH 29-535 PS Q calibre, uniting chronograph precision with a perpetual calendar, and delivers a power reserve of 55–65 hours, finished to the highest standards of Geneva watchmaking.

Patek Philippe

5719/10G-010

The Patek Philippe 5719/10G-010 is rendered in a 40 mm white gold Nautilus case and integrated bracelet, entirely set with baguette-cut diamonds. It is powered by the self-winding calibre 324 S C, offering a power reserve of approximately 35–45 hours, uniting the iconic Gérald Genta design with exceptional high jewellery craftsmanship.

Audemars Piguet

15412BC

The Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Double Balance Wheel Openworked 15412BC presents a 41 mm 18-carat white gold case set with brilliant-cut diamonds. Its openworked dial reveals the self-winding calibre 3132, featuring a patented double balance wheel system and offering a 45-hour power reserve with enhanced precision.

Audemars Piguet

15416CE

The Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Double Balance Wheel Openworked 15416CE is crafted entirely in black ceramic across its 41 mm case and integrated bracelet. It houses the self-winding calibre 3132 with a double balance wheel mechanism, delivering stability, precision, and a 45-hour power reserve.

Rolex

Daytona 6239

The Rolex Daytona 6239 Paul Newman features a 37 mm stainless steel case with an engraved tachymeter bezel and the iconic “exotic” dial. Powered by the manual-winding Valjoux 72 chronograph movement, it stands as one of the most coveted and historically significant Daytona references.
